# Launch video brief

A starting point, not a script. Ben decides the final direction.

## What Keeper is, in one breath

Smart luggage lockers in the places you are already standing when your bag becomes a problem — hotel lobbies, shopping centres, venues. You open one from your phone and pay by the minute, with a daily cap. Starting in Berlin.

## The feeling

The moment your hands are free. Checkout was at 11, the train is at 9pm, and the city is right there. The video should make people feel the weight come off.

## The main splash

`video/MAIN-splash-1080x1920.mp4` is the main splash animation (5s, vertical, 1080×1920, with sound): a teal hill with a winding path rises, then an orange arch settles on top, on cream. Ben wants this one used. The symbol it builds is the Keeper logo. It works as the opening sting or the closing logo moment, and cuts cleanly into the cream end card. A still of the last frame is next to it. The older splash in `video/superseded/` is retired.

## One possible arc (30–45s)

1. **The drag.** A suitcase bumping over cobblestones. Keevy struggling with a heavy case (`keevy-struggle`). "Check-out at 11. Train at 9."
2. **The find.** Phone in hand, a map of the city (`imagery/hands-phone/hand-map.png`, the app wish map).
3. **The drop.** A locker with the warm gradient, one door opens, bag goes in, door closes. Phone shows it locked.
4. **The meter.** A timer ticking, "3c a minute". Then the cap: "Never more than €6 a day."
5. **The freedom.** Golden-hour city, empty hands, friends, bikes (`imagery/lifestyle/`). Keevy waving.
6. **End card.** Ready-made in `end-cards/`. Symbol + **Keeper** · "Explore Your Freedom" · "Starting in Berlin" · keeperlockers.com

## Please don't show or say

These are either not true yet or not confirmed. Ask Ben before using any of them.

- **Real locations or a launch date.** None are published.
- **"Insured", "24/7", "NFC", "QR", "no app needed", "in 30 seconds".** Removed from the site as unproven.
- **Old prices.** Anything showing a €1 unlock fee, "€3 / day", "€5.90", or "€0.05 / min" is outdated. This affects the files in `screenshots/app-prototype-reference/` — use those for layout and motion reference only, and hide or replace the numbers.
- **Customer quotes or reviews.** There are no customers yet.
- **Partner or city names** (the dashboard demo uses a made-up partner and made-up figures).
- **Other companies' branding.**

## About the locker pictures

Every locker photo in `imagery/lockers-ai-illustration/` is **AI-generated**. On the website these carry a visible "Illustration · AI-generated" label, because showing an installation that does not exist as if it were real is a legal problem in Germany (misleading advertising) and under the EU AI Act. If the video uses them, or generates new ones, keep a small on-screen label or make them clearly stylised. Ben will confirm what real locker footage exists — real footage always beats these.

The photos in `imagery/lifestyle-berlin-vertical/` are AI-generated, and those in `imagery/lifestyle/` are, as far as we know, too. Fine as mood and B-roll; Ben to confirm before they carry the video.
